Seasonal Albums

View Genesis throughout the years!

Genesis has been around since the early days of Realms. About once a year, we wipe the realm clean and begin a new world, Each new world is called a season.

Over the years, our community has created an incredible collection of iconic builds. Here, you can browse through albums showcasing past seasons and see how Genesis has grown and evolved over time.

Builds: Our lovely and creative members (Thank you for all your hard work in making Genesis what it is!)
